What G2 ACR is

American Club Racer on the SR II GTS, 1999–2002. Factory Competition Group: stiffer springs, adjustable Konis, 18″ BBS, five-point harnesses, smooth-tube intake, fog-lamp delete, 460 hp / 500 lb-ft vs 450 / 490 on the volume GTS. Comfort Group put the air conditioning and stereo back. About 819 built across four years (215 / 218 / 227 / 159). 2001 is the 227-car year. ABS arrives on 2001. This Kenosha car is advertised as a red/black 2001 with original Konis retained and aftermarket coilovers currently fitted.

It is not a 2002 Final Edition (red with Stone White Le Mans stripes, numbered plaque, 360 GTS coupes of which some were ACR). It is not a 2,400–2,600 mile time-capsule. It is not a fifth-gen ACR Extreme. Those still print mid-six-figures: $445,000 hammer ($467,250 all-in) for a 4,300-mile 2017 on BaT in May 2026, $548,888 hammer ($576,332 all-in) for a 52-mile car in August 2026. That money has not leaked down onto a Kenosha gen-2.

What actually moves the check

As-advertised means original Konis in the deal, miles that hold, and a VIN. Coilovers on with Konis in the box is a story, not a sold. A dealer BaT 10k red ACR flipping from $73,000 to $75,000 hammer is a soft print, not proof ACR trades under GTS — the analog GTS at the same miles did $87,000 hammer. If fluids, tires, or the original-Koni claim are lazy, the $80,000 ask is not cheap. PPI independently, in Kenosha, before anyone writes a check.
