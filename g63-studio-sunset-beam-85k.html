What Studio is

Factory name is AMG Performance Studio Package, about $20,000 new, not a $4–6.5k designo metallic and not Magno. 2016 US colors: Alien Green, Solarbeam, Galacticbeam, Sunsetbeam (code 2173). Gloss-black roof and flares, paint-keyed Exclusive Nappa, carbon. Mutually exclusive with Exclusive Leather. Magno ≠ Studio. Edition 463 ≠ Studio. 2019+ black optics is a different truck.

Zero Sunset Beam solds, any miles, any house. The only Sunset Beam on tape is a 22k truck that no-saled. No stock Studio sold above 36k miles. At 85k this is a high-mile solid-axle G63 that happens to have a $20k factory package.

What actually moves the check

Solid-axle coil-spring W463, not ABC. Missing trans / transfer-case / diff fluid at 85k is real money. Body rust at the spare-tire door, windshield base, rain gutters, or hinges is a walk or a five-figure body bill. Documented 50k fluids and a clean seat can add a few thousand. Salvage is a different book (~$30k).
