What Sunset Beam actually is

Factory name is AMG Performance Studio Package, not a $4–6.5k designo metallic and not Magno. 2016 US brochure colors: Alien Green, AMG Solarbeam, Galacticbeam, Sunsetbeam (paint code 2173). Gloss-black roof, flares, mirrors, bumpers, spare surround. designo Black Exclusive Nappa, stitch keyed to the paint. Carbon interior. Mutually exclusive with the designo Exclusive Leather package.

Original money is about $20,000 for the package. Car and Driver put the four special color-and-trim packages at $20,000 on a $140,825 2016 G63. An 2018 Alien Green window sticker shows $20k Studio Exclusive, $163,795 sticker. German list for sunsetbeam in 2015 was €18,913. Do not mix this with Magno Night Black / Magno Platinum, Edition 463, 2019+ Night Package / black optics, or the other US $20k package (Monza Grey Magno + saddle Exclusive).

Does Studio still print at 85k? Thin Zero Sunset Beam solds at any mileage, any house, in this pull. The only Sunset Beam on BaT is a 22k-mile truck that no-saled at $94,653 in Feb 2024. That is the trophy card, not this one.

How to chop it

  • Keep: 2013–2018 W463 G63 AMG only. Drop 2019+ W463A (independent suspension, different truck), G550, 4x4², 6x6, G65, Brabus G700.
  • Keep: miles. Under 30k trophy / 30–60k / 60–90k driver / 90k+ cheap driver. 85k is the driver band. Do not analog it to a 12k Galactic Beam.
  • Keep: Studio crazy-color vs volume vs designo paint vs Magno. Magno ≠ Studio. Salvage/Copart is a different book.
  • Drop: Edition 463, 2019+ black optics, stacking Exclusive Leather on Studio.
  • Noise: year-within-2013–18, Exclusive interior alone, dealer vs private. Barrett-Jackson ≠ BaT.

The move is not “the $20k package still pays at 85k.” The move is a clean, correctly specced Sunset Beam should not print under a wrecked Magno. $58k sits above the damaged Magno, below the stale damaged gray, $8k over the clean same-year analog. What actually moves the check

These are solid-axle coil-spring W463s. Not ABC. Transfer-case VG150E wants fluid at 50k/5yr; missing trans/TC/diff history is real money at 85k. M157 is labor-heavy. Body rust at the spare-tire door, windshield base, rain gutters, and hinges is a walk or a five-figure body bill. Surface rust on screws is normal. Documented 50k fluids, a recent B service, and a clean seat can add a few thousand. Sub-40k miles is a different card ($70–90k). Salvage is a different book (~$30k).

Gaps

  • No Sunset Beam sold, any miles, any auction house.
  • No Studio sold above 36k miles.
  • Reserve/BaT ingest for G63 titles is thin; this brief is first-party listing pages, not a complete G63 tape.
  • Cars & Bids completed hammers not in this pull. A live 2016 Solar Beam at 35k is mid-auction (high bid $40k as of this writing), not a sold.
  • No official US Studio production count. No 2016 Sunsetbeam window sticker dollar in-hand; $20k is 2016 C&D plus 2018 sticker.
One line An 85k-mile Sunset Beam Studio is a ~$58k driver, not an $89k trophy. The color story is real. The tape has no proof it survives past 36k miles. Price the miles, then pay a small, honest premium for the package, and walk at $68k.
